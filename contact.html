Frequently Asked Store Questions

Do I need an appointment before visiting 123 Mobile House?

No appointment is needed! Walk-ins are always welcome 7 days a week from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM for phone purchases, accessory fitting, or on-the-spot repair diagnostics.

How can I confirm today's price and stock before coming?

Prices can change daily based on official distributor offers. Simply tap the WhatsApp button and ask for today's price on any model — our team will reply with the exact rate and in-stock colors.

Which payment methods do you accept at the counter?

We accept Cash, Fonepay QR, all Nepal Mobile Banking apps, eSewa, and Khalti with no extra surcharge.

Can you transfer my photos and data to my new phone?

Yes, absolutely! When you purchase your new phone at 123 Mobile House, our staff provides free full data transfer (contacts, photos, WhatsApp chats, and apps) from your old phone.
